## Decision

The Audanika plugin plays the app's graph, built from the same code: a
sampler node per MIDI channel 1 to 3 (bass, chord, melody) and `external`,
the modulator with the composed scripts of the combis, delay, plate reverb
and limiter. `PluginBundle` writes `macos/plugin/document.json` (its nodes
carry the default preset, its assets table names all 112 SFZ files) and
`presets.json` (28 presets; every preset names every node, a channel it
does not play gets silence) from `content/sfz/index.json` and the app's
`SfzScript`, `SfzEffects` and `soundPresetName`. A test regenerates both
and fails when they differ from the committed files
(`AUD_UPDATE_PLUGIN=1` rewrites them) and loads all 28 presets into the
headless host. In the plugin MIDI channels 1 to 3 play bass, chord and
melody, every other channel plays `external` (the plan review, answer 1);
the scripts' slider (CC 74 of channel 1) is the control "Slider", since the
scripts compute cutoff, resonance, gain and position themselves.

## Why

- The plugin must sound like the app (req-mac-003); one source of the
  sound rules keeps them from drifting.
- Parameters for cutoff, resonance, gain and position would fight the
  scripts that set them every block; the slider is what a combi offers a
  player.
