## Decision

The SFZ files of the app carry no effect: the compressor that limited
every node at −0.5 dBFS and the +7.35 dB gain effect after it leave
`tool/convert-content-bundle.js` and `content/sfz`. The gain effect only
undid sfizz's synth volume of −7.35 dB, which sfizz applies after the
effect buses; the converter now adds those 7.35 dB to `global_volume`, so
every note keeps its level. The master limiter of app-003 (look-ahead
1.5 ms, ceiling −1 dBFS, dsp-003) holds the sum of all nodes; a single
node may now exceed −0.5 dBFS before it.

## Why

- The per-node limiters ran sfizz's compressor with a `log10` and a `pow`
  per sample on every node, idle ones included: 17 to 25 % of the audio
  thread on the Galaxy Tab S4 before the optimization of sampler-006.
- One limiter on the sum guarantees the ceiling for any number of nodes;
  four limiters at −0.5 dBFS let the sum reach +7.6 dBFS anyway.
