## Decision

On Android `aud_audio_io` uses Oboe directly as its default and only
backend: AAudio with the OpenSL ES fallback below API 27, the latency
tuner, performance modes and sharing modes, channel masks (API 32),
hardware channel count (API 34), MMAP policy queries and device ids
(API 36), the routing-changed callback, the full-duplex helper, and
presentation timestamps and latency for the timing contract. miniaudio
serves macOS, iOS, Windows and Linux and is not built for Android.
Oboe is Apache-2.0 and enters the notices file of the package.

## Why

- Ableton Link alignment (time-001) needs presentation timestamps per
  callback, which Oboe exposes and miniaudio's callback does not.
- Channel masks, hardware channel counts, MMAP and routing callbacks
  are what multi-channel, low-latency IO means on Android (R14).
- Oboe's latency tuner is the difference between a playable instrument
  and glitches on the long tail of Android devices.
- The legacy engine already runs on Oboe; the experience carries over.

## Consequences

- The IO ticket (S3) builds two backends from the start; the former
  Oboe follow-up ticket disappears.
- Oboe's C++ is compiled by the Android build hook; the hook links with
  16 KB page alignment (NDK r28 default).
