## Decision

`tool/convert-soundprism-link-edition.js` rebuilds the 23 visible combis of
SoundPrism Link Edition once into `content/sfz`, in the layout of app-007:
their 44 sample sets from the reduced package as FLAC with SoundPrism's
sample rules, one channel SFZ per channel preset (attack, release, linear
gain and transposition as in app-001; sfizz's 2-pole low-pass per voice),
the combis in `index.json` with SoundPrism's labels and the delay and
reverb of `AUD002021_3_HighReverb` on the master chain. The 69 channel
scripts and the two default scripts ship unchanged in
`content/sfz/scripts`, with the host layer `audanika-host.js`, which does
what SoundPrism's Objective-C interface did: the load order, `sampler.time`
from the beat (`linkTime` = beat × 0.5), CC 74 of the first channel as the
slider, `sampler.update()` per tick and SoundPrism's range rules. It sends
per channel four controls to the sampler of that channel: 300 cutoff
(`cutoff=10`, `cutoff_oncc300=13500` cents), 301 resonance
(`resonance=-20`, `resonance_oncc301=60`), 302 the linear gain
(`amplitude_oncc302=100`) and 303 the pan of the channel
(`position=-100`, `position_oncc303=200`); the pan per note of the scripts
is a `<curve>` over the note number (`pan_oncc133`), built from the
transposed note as SoundPrism passed it, so voice pan and channel pan
multiply as there. `AudSoundEngine` adds one `aud.script.modulator`
(script-001), connects its outputs 0 to 2 to the channel samplers and
output 2 to the external one, connects every external MIDI input to it,
seeks the transport to beat 0 and starts it at 120 BPM at every start of
the engine, and loads a combi's script with its SFZ files: a failed script
fails the load as a failed SFZ file does. The combis appear in the sound
list under their labels; play modes and channels do not change, and the
play-mode variants of the edition are not converted. The developer panel
of app-005 shows the modulator's numbers and a ramp/step switch; the
controls enter as a ramp by default.

## Why

- The scripts carry the sound of the combis; run unchanged, they cannot
  drift from SoundPrism, and a Node reference proves the port per tick.
- The linear gain and `position` cost no extra pass per sample on a ramp,
  and two pan stages reproduce SoundPrism's voice and mixer pan.
- The user keeps the app's play modes and channels (2026-10-18).
