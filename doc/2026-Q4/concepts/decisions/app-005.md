# app-005: The Audanika app governs its sampler voices and remembers the budget per device

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-14
- Canonical source: tickets/2026-10-13-29-limit-the-voices-by-the-cpu-power-of-the-device.md
  (scope 5, decision 4 of the plan review)
- Refines: [app-002](app-002.md) — the sampler nodes per channel
- Open work: none

## Decision

The app's `AudSoundEngine` adds an `AudLoadGovernor` (lifecycle-003) over
the voice budget of its sampler nodes before the engine starts; the
polyphony of each node stays at 64. The governor starts from the budget
the app learned on this device before — kept in the shared preferences per
`AudDeviceClass.key`, platform and model, so that a backup restored on
another device starts from that device's class — and the app writes the
budget once the governor has settled, at most every 10 s. Players see
nothing new. A press of five seconds on the Sounds page shows or hides a
developer panel with the budget, the voices, the stolen voices per minute,
the load (mean, p99, maximum) and the xruns; the setting is kept, its
strings are English only.

## Why

- The first start on a device learns its budget; later starts begin there
  and do not drop out while the governor settles again.
- A developer needs to see the governor on a device without a debugger;
  players need no setting for it (decision 4 of ticket 29).
