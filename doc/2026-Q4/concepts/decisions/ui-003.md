# ui-003: The engine adapters of the UI packages live in a binding package

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Canonical source: tickets/2026-10-09-24-spike-a-flutter-editor-for-the-plugin-shells.md
  (decision 3 of the plan review)
- Supersedes in part: the adapter placement of [ui-001](ui-001.md) and
  [ui-002](ui-002.md) (the adapters to `aud_audio` in the UI packages);
  their other decisions stand
- Open work: S17a creates `aud_audio_ui_bindings` with the parameter sink
  over `AudEngine`; S17b adds `NoteBinding`; the graph editor, the piano
  roll and the waveform view add theirs when they are built

## Decision

The UI packages (`aud_audio_ui_controls`, `aud_audio_ui_keyboard`,
`aud_audio_ui_graph_edit`, `aud_audio_ui_piano_roll`,
`aud_audio_ui_waveform`) do not depend on the engine. Their widgets bind to
abstract sinks and models of their own: `AudParamBinding` sends to an
`AudParamSink` (begin and end a gesture, set a plain value, values from
outside), as the spike built it in `aud_audio_ui_controls` 0.1.0. The
adapters that connect these sinks and models to `AudEngine` of the umbrella
`aud_audio` live in one binding package, `aud_audio_ui_bindings`, which
S17a creates. It depends on the umbrella and on the UI packages; apps
depend on it. Plugin editors do not: they implement the sinks over their
shell's protocol (the socket sink of `aud_audio_vst3`) or the parameter
tree of the Audio Unit (`aud_audio_auv3`).

## Why

- A dependency on the umbrella builds the graph, the audio IO and `jni`
  into every app that only shows a knob, and the umbrella declares iOS and
  Android only: a macOS editor process cannot depend on it at all.
- The spike's editors bind the same widgets to a socket (VST3 on macOS) and
  to an `AUParameterTree` (AUv3 on iOS) through the sink; the engine
  adapter is a third implementation of the same interface.
- The umbrella stays free of Flutter UI, the UI packages stay testable
  without native builds, and the binding package is the one place that
  follows changes of both.
