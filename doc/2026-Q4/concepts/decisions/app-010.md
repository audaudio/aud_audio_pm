## Decision

The editor is Audanika itself in the profile `AppProfile.pluginEditor`: the
`Application` without audio engine, MIDI devices, store, migrations and
route saving, its preferences in memory per view. A `PluginEditorView`
shows the play surface with the studio, the Sounds page and the library. Its `ShellSoundEngine` plays nothing: it sends the play surface's
MIDI 1.0 bytes to the plugin (`AudShellMidi`) and the preset the Sounds page
chooses (`AudShellSelectPreset`). The plugin plays the editor's notes in its
graph at the start of the next block on the channels of the DAW's MIDI -
1 bass, 2 chord, 3 melody - and sends them to the DAW on its event output
"MIDI Out" (16 channels): notes and poly pressure as VST3 events, control
changes, channel pressure and pitch bend as legacy MIDI CC events. A preset
the editor chooses is an edit of the parameter "Preset" (begin, perform,
end), which the DAW records; a preset the DAW chooses, automates or loads
with a project reaches the editor (`AudShellPreset`), and the Sounds page
follows it.

- **Library (2026-10-22).** The editor shows the library with the free
  songs and the albums the user bought in Audanika; other albums stay
  locked, and buying stays in the app. StoreKit shows the editor, an app of
  its own, none of the app's purchases, so the app writes the product IDs
  of its purchases into the defaults of the app group
  `SAZSC68S46.com.audanika`, which the app and the editor carry, and the
  editor reads them.

## Why

- The plan review, answer 2: the surface's notes go to the DAW, so that a
  track records what the player plays in the editor.
- req-mac-004: the plugin's editor is Audanika's play surface with its
  sound selection.
- req-mac-003: the plugin plays the same presets as the app; the editor
  plays them through the plugin's graph, never through an engine of its
  own.
- One code base: the editor is the app's own play surface, not a copy.
- Gabriel Gatzsche's request of 2026-10-22: the library in the editor as
  in the app, the free and the bought albums ("Free and owned albums").
- Measured with the test host: a tap on the chord button "C" sends C2 on
  channel 1; a tap into the row G sends B on channel 3 and D, G and B on
  channel 2.
