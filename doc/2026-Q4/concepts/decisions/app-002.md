## Decision

The app's engine holds one sampler node per MIDI channel the current
preset maps (bass 0, chord 1, melody 2) and one node `external`. The
on-screen keys, the play hints and the motion sensors produce MIDI 1.0
bytes as before; a router on the main isolate turns them into UMP events
and sends each channel voice message to the node of its channel through
`graph.sendEvent`. Unmapped channels are silent. CC 64 goes to every
channel node, as the legacy hold did; CC 120 and CC 123 become note offs
for every sounding note, so Stop releases the notes with their release
time instead of sfizz's hard cut; CC 7 stays on its channel. A channel
that a new preset does not map gets an empty instrument. External MIDI
opens every input that is not the app's own, through `AudMidiBridge`, and
plays the node `external`, which holds the melody sound of the preset on
every channel; the bridge uses the native tap where the backend has one.
A failed load keeps the previous sound.

## Why

- The legacy app played three sounds on three channels; sfizz has no
  channels (app-001).
- An external keyboard sends on channel 1 by default; without its own
  node it would play the contra bass.
- sfizz cuts all voices on CC 120 and CC 123; the legacy engine released
  them, and the app sends CC 123 on every Stop.
- The router runs on the main isolate and only enqueues events; no Dart
  runs on the audio thread, and external MIDI never touches Dart.
